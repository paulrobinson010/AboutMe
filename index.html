About

I'm Paul — a maker who can't leave a hobby alone. If I run, ride, play or throw it, sooner or later I build an app for it.

Every project here started the same way: doing something I enjoy and thinking "this would be better with a scoreboard." TreadGame exists to make the morning treadmill run something to actually look forward to. CycleHUD grew from wanting to know about every car behind me without taking my eyes off the road. DartsScorer lets a camera do the pub maths, out loud. And It's Just a Game — because the best games are the ones everyone in the room can play.
