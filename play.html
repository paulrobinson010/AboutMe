Play.

Little games and tools for your phone. Free, no ads, no sign-up, and nothing stored.

  • Putt. Mini golf scorecard Tap in everyone's shots, hole by hole, and watch the leaderboard shuffle. A big crowd? Split into groups and score on several phones, or let others watch live. Any number of playersScore on several phones
  • Spin. Decision wheel Can't decide where to eat, or who's paying? Pop the options on the wheel, give it a flick, and let it choose. As many options as you likeOne phone
  • Imposter. Party word game Everyone gets the secret word except the imposter, who has to bluff along. Talk it round, then vote out whoever you think is faking it. 3+ playersPass one phone, or everyone's own
  • Noughts. Noughts and crosses Glow-in-the-dark noughts and crosses. Take on Robo at three levels, or try Vanishing, where only your last three marks stay. vs Robo2 players, 1 phone2 phones
  • Battleships. Sink the fleet Hide your fleet, then hunt theirs one bomb at a time. Take on Robo from Cadet to Admiral, pass one phone between you, or play phone to phone. vs Robo2 players, 1 phone2 phones
